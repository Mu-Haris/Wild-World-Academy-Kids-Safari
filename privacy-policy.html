🛡️Our Commitment to Privacy

Wild World Academy Kids Safari ("we", "our", or "the App") is a family-friendly educational trivia game. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, how it is used, and the choices you have.

📋Information We Collect Directly

We do not collect any personally identifiable information directly.

The App does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, contact information, or precise location data.

All gameplay data — including quiz progress, scores, unlocked content, and your sound and accessibility preferences — is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers.

📢Advertising and Tracking

The free version of the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob is a third-party service that may collect device-level information for the purpose of serving and measuring advertisements.

Information that AdMob may collect includes:

  • Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only when you grant tracking permission (see below)
  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • General (non-precise) location based on IP address
  • App interaction events used to measure ad performance
  • Crash diagnostics

For more information about how Google handles advertising data, please visit the Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads Policy.

🔐App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, the App will ask for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You will see a system prompt shortly after launching the App that says:

"Allow Kids Safari to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?"

  • If you choose "Allow": Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads and measure their effectiveness.
  • If you choose "Ask App Not to Track": The IDFA is not shared. AdMob will serve only non-personalized ads. The App remains fully functional.

You can change your decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

🌐Tracking Domains

For transparency, the following domains may be contacted by the AdMob SDK for advertising and tracking purposes (when permission is granted):

  • googleads.g.doubleclick.net
  • googleadservices.com
  • googlesyndication.com

💳In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase ("Premium Unlock") that removes advertisements and unlocks additional content. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

👨‍👩‍👧Use by Children

The App contains family-friendly educational content suitable for a wide audience. The App is rated 4+ on the App Store. The App is not classified in Apple's "Made for Kids" / Kids Category, and is not designed exclusively for children.

If a child uses the App, we recommend that a parent or guardian:

  • Decide how to respond to the App Tracking Transparency prompt on the child's behalf
  • Manage in-app purchases via Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy features

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can investigate.

🔗Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchases. Privacy Policy
